# Mortal simulation rules

- A save begins at age zero. Its starting year, starting values, and generated family are stored alongside its seed. Given the same snapshot and sequence of typed actions, outcomes are reproducible.
- Age Up advances the year, ages living parents, resets energy and annual action usage, applies school and health progression, processes delayed effects, and checks contextual events. At most one new contextual event is triggered per year.
- A mandatory decision blocks other simulation actions until resolved. Each contextual event can trigger once per life. Some events have guaranteed age milestones, and optional events use seeded probability with contextual conditions and modifiers.
- Attributes and each relationship dimension stay between 0 and 100. The journal records the applied change after clamping. Balances use integer cents. Transactions account for every change to the bank balance.
- School, career, and relationship actions retain their own requirements and annual limits. Personal activities can be repeated without spending energy. Parents age independently of these interactions.
- Natural decline and seeded mortality can end a life in later years. The maximum age is 115. The final state retains its journal and can be opened from Saved Lives.

## Education

- Primary starts at six, secondary at twelve, and high school at fifteen. High school requires performance of at least 35 and attendance of at least 45 to graduate. Postsecondary programs require 45 in both. Players who leave school early or fail high school can apply for one year of tuition-free high school equivalency as adults.
- Grades are independent of Smarts. The annual calculation blends prior performance, Smarts, Discipline, attendance, health, low academic stress, a matching aptitude bonus, and seeded variation. Activities are available once per year and affect grades, attendance, or stress as well as energy. A new school stage or program begins with its own performance and attendance.
- The first high school qualification triggers a mandatory decision pop-up. The gap year delays applications by one calendar year. The other choices set intent and leave the player free to change direction later.
- Admission checks qualifications and program prerequisites. The score weights the relevant previous grade at 56%, Smarts at 26%, and Discipline at 18%, plus seven points for a matching aptitude. A seeded roll from −7 to +5 determines offers around the program threshold. Offers expire after two years and can be accepted only while the player is unenrolled.
- Tuition for the first year is due at enrollment. Later terms are charged as they open if fully funded. If funding is insufficient, study and academic-year progression pause while the rest of the life continues. Eligible students can receive one merit or need-based scholarship per enrollment. Each funded year records its own scholarship credit and tuition debit. Money already paid stays in the ledger when the player withdraws.
- School peers age into Graduate at 18. Program students graduate after their cohort's expected duration. They spend the following year marked Job Seeker and then become Employed. These are occupation labels. Friendships survive stage changes, and only living contacts can interact.

## Work and finance

- Employment opens at 18, with one active contract. A vacancy stays stable for the current calendar year and each application can be attempted once. Offers expire at Age Up. Specialized jobs check both the required level and relevant tags on a matching credential, plus industry experience. Hiring depends on grades where available, relevant attributes, experience, reputation, competition, and a seeded roll.
- A player can work part-time while studying. Working more than 16 hours a week reduces attendance and raises academic stress. Full-time work and enrollment are mutually exclusive. Adjusting hours changes workload while the contracted salary stays fixed until a raise, promotion, or new job.
- Annual review depends on performance and security. Two consecutive warnings can end employment. Promotions also require a listed path, fit for the new role, reputation, and a seeded opportunity. Salaries stay within the role's band. One raise request is allowed per year.
- Actions between birthdays take effect at the start of the current simulation year. The contract active at the next Age Up earns the previous year's pay, so quitting, replacing the job, or retiring before the boundary removes that contract from the next payroll. Settlement records the earned salary and bills in the new calendar year, including the final elapsed adult year at death.
- Age Up advances the calendar, resets annual limits, applies natural health changes, and ages living NPCs. It then settles prior-year payroll, income tax, older arrears, and new living bills, records elapsed experience, and expires offers. Illness progression runs after settlement. Death closes a job. If the player survives, academic workload, grades and tuition are processed before reviews, promotions or dismissal, delayed outcomes, and the contextual event. The repository saves the resulting turn atomically.
- All amounts use integer cents in the existing currency. Salary above $240 is taxed at 8%, with an additional 8% above $700 and 5% above $1,300. Housing varies by city. Food, utilities, transportation, and personal costs are recorded separately. Unpaid essential costs carry forward by category, and wages can clear those bills on a later turn.
- The workplace roster keeps the same contact IDs across saves and job changes. Former co-worker friends remain available as friends. Current work discussions can improve performance. Insulting or pranking a current colleague hurts job security or reputation in addition to the relationship.

## Health and events

- Age and health influence the seeded chance of new illness. One active case per patient can improve, persist, or worsen over later years. Untreated illness can lower health and the player's energy.
- Each city has three doctors. Higher reputation carries a higher fee and a higher probability of successful treatment. Severity lowers that probability. Successful treatment clears a mild or moderate case and stabilizes a severe case to moderate. The selected fee is charged for the visit even if the case persists.
- Health visits can repeat if the patient and balance still qualify. Emergency care guarantees stabilization for a severe illness, and the optometrist treats eye strain. For players under 18, the community doctor, emergency care, and the optometrist are free. To accompany a living family member or friend, the player must have received salary and have enough cash. Visits that NPCs arrange independently do not charge the player.
- Psychiatry reduces stress and improves happiness. Eligible players can repeat blood and plasma donations without spending energy. The first plasma donation each year adds compensation to other income in the ledger.
- Decisions block gameplay until answered. Informational pop-ups wait behind any decision and are acknowledged in journal order. Their journal entries remain available afterward.

The headless runner varies education, employment, and doctor visits across seeded lives. It checks salary bounds, contract eligibility, ledger and tax reconciliation, unique payments, graduation dates, save round-trips, and termination.

## Library and property

- The first three library visits in a year can roll for an encounter, with at most one encounter that year. Shared-hobby encounters can add a friend and can happen again in a later year.
- Renting, asking a parent for a home, and buying assets become available at 18. A housing request checks the parent's available funds and uses the relationship and seeded roll to determine consent. A gifted starter home becomes the player's residence automatically.
- Each purchase debits the full in-game price and creates one owned item. Players can move into any house they own. The annual housing bill uses the current residence, from a family contribution or rent to maintenance for an owned home.

## Social and family progression

- Age Up preserves the order of player payroll, tax, expenses, and education. The living-cost pass charges childcare once per year for living dependents through the year they turn 18. Unpaid childcare is personal-expense arrears.
- Parent support debits their savings. Up to 18 important NPCs receive annual progression, with relatives and current partners prioritized. Promotions require an available career path and suitable education. Retired NPCs use a small independent income and basic expenses.
- Friendship tiers use familiarity over time, the six bond attributes, personality, and recent memories. A neglected bond can become a former friendship. Romantic advances and family planning use deterministic annual rolls and cooldowns. Refusal still consumes that year’s attempt.
- Adults can receive one introduction a year through Activities → Love. A casual date leaves the player and the match as acquaintances. Concurrent dating is allowed, with a single engagement or marriage at a time. During Age Up, discovery depends on the number of active partners and the discovering partner’s trust. If discovered, that partner leaves and the response affects trust and stress. Divorce uses the existing cash settlement.
- An engagement opens three cash-paid wedding options. Divorce transfers 8% of the player’s balance up to $50 to the former spouse. Child and property IDs are retained. Parenting uses age-appropriate actions and modifies learning, wellbeing, confidence, and trust.
- Mandatory life decisions take precedence over NPC invitations. An invitation stores its participant and applies its response before clearing the decision. A major promotion, relocation, or death may create an informational notice.
- Walks, library visits, and psychiatry remain repeatable. Their incremental benefits taper with repetition. Clinical visits remain available for eligible illnesses.

## Skills and lifestyle

- Aptitudes are determined by the life seed and existing natural talent. Practice gains depend on aptitude, proficiency, method, discipline, health, stress, consistency, and relevant attributes. Physical training slows later in life. Free practice uses energy and remains available alongside paid classes, courses, and coaching.
- Each calendar year permits 16 formal sessions in total, at most five for each skill. Each skill can gain up to 18 proficiency points from all sources in a year. Rest restores energy while the training budget remains tied to Age Up. Low health can block sports practice. Repeated exertion while stressed or unwell can reduce health.
- A skill records experience and personal best separately from current proficiency. Small training gains accumulate between sessions. Physical proficiency can start to decay after four years of inactivity and other skills after six. Physical decay is faster after 60. Retention floors are 30% of personal best for physical skills and 55% for other skills. Education, work, and reading can maintain or raise appropriate skills within their source limits.
- One amateur event is available per year across all skills. Entry requires age eligibility, three formal sessions, at least 25% proficiency, and recent practice. Scores combine proficiency, training history, conditioning, and a seeded roll. Fees and lessons use integer cents and are posted at the time of the action.
- New lifestyle actions give tapered effects during their first three uses per year. Further use has no new fee or journal entry. Walk benefits flatten after six visits. The public library grants small Writing practice on its first four visits while retaining the original encounter rolls. After six uses, uneventful walks and library visits leave the save unchanged.
- A child gets up to three interests keyed to their own tastes and age. School and confidence shape incidental growth. Encouragement and paid lessons are limited by the action’s annual cooldown and available balance.

## Living world

- Yearly world conditions combine persistent economic regimes, prior values, sector trends, city specializations, and occasional shocks. Crisis lasts at most three consecutive years. National growth, inflation, unemployment, and interest are stored in basis points. The consumer price index compounds annually and is bounded between 72 and 250 from a baseline of 100.
- Payroll and taxes use the contract held as the year ends. Essential expenses and childcare use the elapsed year’s price index and local conditions. Existing arrears are paid at their recorded balances. Tax thresholds, tuition, medical fees, and training fees retain their nominal amounts.
- Vacancies and offers stay stable for the year. Local employers receive priority, and commuting to another city raises transport costs. Entry-level roles remain available during recessions. Employer demand affects hiring, salary reviews, promotions, and economic redundancy. Redundancy and poor-performance dismissal remain separate outcomes.
- Relocation opens at 18, costs money, and is available once per year after leaving active employment and enrollment. A spouse and minor dependent children move with the player. The household rents on arrival. Homes retain their original location, and vehicles retain their ownership and acquisition records.
- New house prices use both the national price index and the city housing market. Vehicle prices use inflation and a smaller local adjustment. Parents pay the exact current quote when they grant a home. Balance, cash history, and asset acquisition amounts retain their nominal values.
- NPCs settle bills against elapsed local prices. Current conditions shape new salaries, job prospects, retirement income, and the risk of job loss. Updates retain the existing annual limit. News is saved separately from personal notifications and never takes the mandatory decision slot.

## Wealth, banking, and asset management

- Each Age Up pays elapsed salary and tax, then settles rental income and tenant turnover, pension contributions or benefits, savings interest, and bond distributions. It pays prior living arrears and current living and childcare bills before personal debt. The world advances once. Housing and fund prices then reflect the new year, and the estate snapshot includes that year’s personal outcomes and any death settlement.
- Net worth is cash plus savings, funds, and active property and vehicles valued at current market prices, less unpaid living bills, loan principal, and accrued unpaid interest. Overdue principal is already included in the outstanding balance. The pension is an annual benefit. The ledger tracks past benefit payments as cash when received.
- Adult savings transfers move cash at face value. The annual deposit rate is 66% of the national rate minus 0.35 percentage points, bounded from 0.45% to 5.20%. A loan permits one application per year and one outstanding loan. Fixed offers expire at the next Age Up. Creditworthiness considers income, stability, arrears, prior applications, available savings and cash, and repayment history. Affordability reserves for essential bills, dependents, and commuting.
- Personal loan interest is calculated on principal each year. Repayment covers accrued interest first and then principal. The final installment covers the exact balance and interest in cents. Two consecutive shortfalls put a loan in default. Principal and unpaid interest remain due, and unpaid interest does not compound. Early repayment uses cash and is available once per year.
- House values use the property’s city, current national index, condition, and bounded improvement value. Vehicles use their acquisition city, condition, and bounded age depreciation. The sale deduction is 5% for a house and 6.5% for a vehicle. Current residences and homes listed for rent must be vacated or delisted before sale.
- Owned residences pay the existing civic-services portion of housing bills. Optional asset upkeep and rental turnover are separate costs. Unattended assets wear down but remain owned. Listed homes have an annual occupancy roll linked to local conditions and condition, and rent and turnover post in occupied years. At most one reliable local vehicle reduces annual transport costs, by up to $4.50.
- Eligible payroll contributes 2% of salary from the employee and credits another 1.1% from the employer. The pension starts after retirement at 65 or later and at least five paid contribution years. The benefit uses up to 15 recent salary credits and a capped replacement rate and payment.
- Fund prices are fixed within a year and follow the saved national or sector indicators. Trades use whole units and cents. Sales deduct 1% rounded up and release the proportional average cost basis. The government bond fund pays an annual cash distribution tied to the national interest rate.
